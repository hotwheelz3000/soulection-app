## Soulection Radio 5.0

**New**
- **Picks up where you left off, like Spotify:** if the app was closed (by iOS or by you), it opens with the song you were on in the mini player, paused, at the same second. Tap play and it carries on: the show, or Likes, History or Mixtape with the same songs coming up.
- **Forward button** next to Back in the top bar: goes forward again after going back (dimmed when there's nowhere to go).
- **Backup on the iPhone, always:** Likes, History and Continue listening are saved in Files → On My iPhone → Soulection after every change. Nothing to set up.
- **Continue listening is backed up:** it's in the export file and the automatic backups, and comes back with an import or restore.
- **Top bar on every page:** where the site builds its top bar differently (show and song pages right now), the app's own bar covers it, with a Search button that opens the home page's search, ready to type.
- **Build numbers:** after the first build of a version, the download is named with its build number (e.g. `SoulectionRadio-5.0-24.ipa`) and Help shows "(build 24)".
- **New-show alerts more often:** the app asks iOS to check every 15 minutes, checks every 30 minutes while music plays in the background, and checks when you leave the app. Help explains the two settings that make alerts work when the app is closed.

**Fixes**
- **Smoother long scrolls:** the list of shows loads the next batch about two screens early, so a fast scroll rarely stops on "Loading more episodes…". Covers coming up start loading right away, so they show sooner.
- **Dynamic Island keeps the cover:** in other apps it no longer falls back to a plain speaker icon when the song changes (the app now hands iOS the cover itself instead of a link to download).
- **Dice while a show plays** (also one started from its page): the new show takes over and stays; if it doesn't start, you get a message instead of nothing. The first tap is quicker too.
- **Timestamps** jump to their song again, and a long tap no longer selects text.
- **Search:** the top bar stays on one line after a search, with suggestions opening right under it.
- **iPhone home page:** the Trending tracks row is hidden; Featured episodes go straight into All episodes.
- **Back** works on the home page as soon as there's a page to go back to.
- **Swipe back** on the mini player always plays the song before (the big player's back button still restarts the song first).
- **No accidental resets:** pulling down at the top no longer reloads the page and stops the music. If the app opens without a connection, it retries by itself.
- **iCloud backup:** the reconnect reminder comes at most once a week, shows iOS's reason, and the app re-checks its link to the file after saving.
- **No shading** on a show card when a scroll starts on it.
- **Messages** are a little easier to read.
- **Cleanup:** old code removed; Likes, Sleep timer and Help share one panel, so all three close with the same smooth pull-down.

## Soulection Radio 4.0

- **Continue listening:** in Likes (History tab), the shows you were in the middle of. Tap one to pick it back up where you were.
- **Dice while a show plays** switches like Mixtape: the new show starts muted next to the old one, then takes over. It never picks the show you're already hearing.
- **Big player:** opens by growing out of the mini player and closes back into it, without shaking. It stays open when you leave the app and come back, and swiping up to leave no longer opens it.
- **Up Next:** smooth scrolling, the arrow centred under the show name, long song names wrap.
- **Likes panel:** pulls down smoothly, closes with a flick, and has a line between History and Likes.
- **Backup through updates:** updating no longer turns backup off; **Reconnect** or **New backup** if iOS asks.
- **Feels more like an app:** the app's own screens can't be highlighted; less lag in Mixtape; the mini player always shows the song you're hearing; top bar buttons hold their spot between pages.

## Earlier versions

- **Up Next** in the big player: the songs coming up, or the show's songs. Tap one to play it, or its heart to like it.
- **Big player** slides up in one swipe and back down to close. **Swipe the mini player** to skip.
- **Starting a show:** the logo spins in the play button until the music starts.
- **Top bar** with the same buttons on every page: Back and Home on the left, your buttons on the right.
- **Dice** plays a random show right where you are. **Backup** turns on by saving one file; after a reinstall, pick that file.
