## Soulection Radio 5.0

A maintenance build: nothing new to learn, just tidier and quicker.

- **Trending on iPhone:** the home page shows Trending artists and Trending tracks at the top, side by side like on iPad (on the phone they were hidden below the endless list of shows).
- **Forward button:** next to Back in the top bar; goes forward again after going back (dimmed when there's nowhere to go).
- **Continue listening is backed up:** it's now in the export file and the automatic backups, and comes back with an import or restore.
- **Dice while a show from its page plays:** the switch now holds even when the site's own player tries to carry on, and if it doesn't take, you get a message instead of nothing.
- **Quicker dice:** the first tap no longer waits on extra requests to the site; the full list of shows comes straight from the site's database.
- **No more accidental resets:** pulling down at the top of a page no longer reloads it (that stopped the music and reset the player). If the app opens without a connection, it tries loading again by itself.
- **No more shading** on a show card when a scroll or pull starts on it (tracklist rows keep their press highlight).
- **Backup you never have to reconnect:** your Likes and History are now also saved on the iPhone itself (Files → On My iPhone → Soulection), after every change. If the iCloud copy pauses, the reminder comes at most once a week instead of on every launch, and shows iOS's reason. The app also re-checks its link to the iCloud file after saving, so it should stop pausing.
- **Swipe back goes back:** swiping the mini player right now always plays the song before (the back button in the big player still restarts the song first, like Spotify).
- **New-show alerts more often:** the app asks iOS to check every 15 minutes (it was an hour), checks every 30 minutes while music plays in the background, and checks when you leave the app. Help explains the two settings that make alerts work when the app is closed.
- **Build numbers:** after the first build of a version, the download is named with its build number (e.g. SoulectionRadio-5.0-12.ipa) and Help shows "(build 12)", so each download has its own name.
- **Timestamps:** tapping a timestamp jumps to that song again (taps were being held back), and a long tap no longer selects text.
- **Top bar on every page:** on pages where the site builds its top bar differently (show and song pages right now), the app's own bar covers it, with Back, Home, a Search button (opens the home page's search, ready to type) and all the other buttons in their usual spots.
- **Back on the home page:** Back lights up and works as soon as there's a page to go back to (it's only dimmed right after opening the app).
- **Easier to read messages:** the text in the app's messages is a little bigger.
- **Cleanup:** leftover code from old builds removed (the old floating Likes tab and one-time settings cleanups), and the Likes, Sleep timer and Help panels now share one piece of code, so Sleep timer and Help get the same smooth pull-down to close as Likes.

## Soulection Radio 4.0

**New**
- **Continue listening:** in Likes (History tab), a row of the last few shows you were in the middle of, with how much is left. Tap one to pick it back up right where you were, without leaving the page. A show counts after 20 seconds (dice shows too), and until there's one the row says what will appear there.
- **Leaving the app:** swiping up from the bottom to leave the app or open the app switcher no longer opens the big player, and a big player that's open stays open when you come back.
- **Show pages:** a sideways drag on the song list no longer moves it (the show's player is left untouched, so timestamps play as before).
- **Dice while a show plays:** switches the same way Mixtape does (the new show starts muted next to the old one, then takes over), so it no longer spins forever.
- **Closing the big player** lands exactly on the mini player: no shake at the end, and the tab rides down with it instead of popping in afterwards.
- **Dice:** spins as soon as you tap it, and a try that got stuck can't block it any more. Starting Likes or Mixtape cancels a dice try that's still waiting, so it can't pop up a message or interrupt Mixtape later.
- **Likes panel:** pulling it down follows your finger smoothly (the list no longer bounces against it), a quick flick closes it, and a thin line separates History and Likes.
- **Feels more like an app:** text on the app's own screens (welcome, help, player, Likes, messages) can't be highlighted any more, so no Copy / Look Up pop-ups. The site's pages work as before.
- **Less lag (Mixtape especially):** the automatic backup is saved in the background, so it never holds up the app.
- **Opening the big player (from 3.0):** the card grows out of the mini player. The mini player's song row rides up and fades, the cover grows from the little picture into the big one, and the rest comes in at the end. Closing does the same in reverse.
- **Up Next:** the arrow back to the cover sits centred under the show name, and long song names wrap onto the next line instead of running under the heart.
- **Mini player:** always shows the song you're hearing, also after jumping to a song (the site's own line used to stay on the song before).
- **Backup through updates:** updating the app no longer turns backup off. If iOS asks, tap **Reconnect**: pick your backup file, then save it back to the same folder (Save, then Replace). Or tap **New backup** to save a fresh one. Any file can be picked now (backups used to show greyed out).
- **Dice while a show plays:** the show that's on keeps playing until the random one has started, then stops (the logo spins meanwhile), the same way Likes and Mixtape switch shows. Tapping again cancels the earlier try, so shows no longer skip. It also never picks the show you're already hearing, and if a show doesn't start it says so and the dice works again.
- **Up Next scrolls smoothly:** swiping inside the list only scrolls it (swipe its header or tap the arrow to close), and it slides in a touch slower. Closing the player from Up Next just shrinks the card, with the list fading, and the player opens on the cover next time.
- **Top bar:** the buttons no longer jump when you go to another page. They hold their spot while the new page loads.

**From 2.0**

**New in the player**
- **Up Next:** tap Up Next in the big player. In Likes, History or Mixtape it lists the songs coming up; in a show, the show's songs. Tap a song to play it, or its heart to like it.
- **One swipe:** the big player slides up from the bottom in one swipe (there's a tab on the mini player again) and slides back down to close.
- **Swipe to skip:** swipe the mini player left for the next song, right for the previous one.
- **Starting a show:** the Soulection logo spins in the play button until the music starts. If it doesn't start, the app quietly tries again.

**Getting around**
- The top bar has the same buttons on every page, staying put with the search box: Back and Home on the left, your buttons on the right. Back goes back a page (dimmed when there isn't one), so you'll never need to restart the app to find your way.
- Show pages no longer slide sideways while you scroll.

**Also**
- The dice plays a random show right where you are, without opening its page.
- Backup: turn it on by saving one backup file; after a reinstall, just pick that file. Your songs come back and backup carries on, with no extra save step.
- Show covers keep their space while the list loads, so scrolling doesn't jump.
