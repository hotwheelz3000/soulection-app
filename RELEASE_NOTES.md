## Soulection Radio 1.0

The first full release of the unofficial Soulection Radio app for iPhone and iPad.

**Listening**
- Keeps playing with the screen off, with the song, artist and cover on the lock screen and in the Dynamic Island.
- Welcome player: "This is Soulection Radio." Tap play to start the newest show right away.
- Mini player and full player, with a heart to like the song playing, AirPlay and a sleep timer.
- Smoother switches between shows in Likes, History and Mixtape, including with the screen off.

**Likes and History**
- Heart any song. Play your Likes or History in order or shuffled, search them, and share songs or find them on Spotify and Apple Music.

**Extras**
- Random show, endless Mixtape, sleep timer with +5 min, and new-show notifications.

**Backup**
- One step: choose a folder (iCloud Drive is best). After a reinstall, choose the same folder to get everything back.

**Help**
- Tap ? for what each button does. Show welcome screen brings the welcome screen back.
